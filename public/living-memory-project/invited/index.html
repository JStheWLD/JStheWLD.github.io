You’re invited.

to 21 Conversations about the Nashville A-Team

The stories behind the records, told by the people who know best.

The stories behind Nashville’s records are disappearing.

Most of them were never written down.

We’re asking the people in and around the rooms to tell them.

And we’d like to hear from you.

HOW IT WORKS

21 conversations. Two or three people at a time.

With the people in and around the rooms, and sometimes interested parties.

WHAT WE ARE ASKING

One conversation. About half an hour.

You can go longer if you want. A video call with two or three people: you, someone from the Living Memory team, and sometimes interested parties.

HOW THE CALL WORKS

It’s not an interview. It’s a conversation.

What you remember gets mapped in the Living Memory, joined to the people, records and sessions you talk about.

You get a link by email. Click it at the time, and you’re on the call.

Before anything goes up, a person on our team reads it.

You can cancel or stop at any time. No reason needed.
