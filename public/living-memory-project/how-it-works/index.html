Meet Maybelle.

An oral history AI. Maybelle finds the people who were there, brings them together, and keeps what they remember.

Asks like a reporter. Listens like a neighbor. Says up front that Maybelle is an AI.

WHAT IS NEW

Oral history used to wait for someone to show up.

THE USUAL WAYWITH MAYBELLE
You find someone to talk to.Maybelle finds the people who were there.
You write the questions.Maybelle asks the next good question.
The recording goes into an archive.Every story joins the Living Memory, checked.
That’s the end of it.Every conversation names the next people.
